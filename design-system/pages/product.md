# Product Page - Design Overrides

> **Overrides Master:** Yes

## Layout

- Full-width image gallery on left (60%)
- Product info on right (40%)

---

## Typography

- Product title: H2 (2.5rem)
- Price: Body LG with accent color
- Description: Body (1rem)
- Meta info: Caption (0.75rem)

---

## Colors

- Sale price: #DC2626 (red)

- Out of stock: #78716C (muted)
